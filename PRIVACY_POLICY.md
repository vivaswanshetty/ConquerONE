# Privacy Policy for ConquerONE

**Last Updated:** August 29, 2026

Welcome to **ConquerONE** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a transparent and secure experience while using our mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights regarding your data.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

#### A. Account & Profile Information
When you register or sign in to ConquerONE (via Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or email authentication), we collect:
- Your name / display name
- Email address
- Profile photo URL (optional)
- Unique User Identifier (Firebase Auth UID)

#### B. Health & Fitness Data (Android Health Connect & Apple Health)
With your explicit in-app consent, ConquerONE integrates with **Android Health Connect** and **Apple HealthKit** to read and/or write the following metrics:
- **Steps**
- **Heart Rate**
- **Active Calories Burned & Total Calories Burned**
- **Basal Metabolic Rate**
- **Distance**

**Health Connect Compliance Notice:**
- Health data is used **strictly** to display real-time workout stats, calculate energy expenditure, and track your fitness progress within the app.
- We **never sell** your health data to any third party, broker, or data marketplace.
- We **never use** health data for targeted advertising, credit scoring, or financial profiling.
- We do not transfer your health data to third parties except as required to provide core app functionality (e.g., secure encrypted cloud synchronization).

#### C. Workout Logs & Progress Metrics
- Custom and preset workout routines, completed sets, repetitions, weights/mass units (kg/lbs), rest intervals, timestamps, and streak statistics.

#### D. Device & Notification Preferences
- Notification schedules for workout reminders.
- Device state preferences (e.g., persistent display mode, sound/haptic cues).

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
1. **Core App Functionality**: Providing customized workout tracking, streak monitoring, widget synchronization, and fitness performance logs.
2. **Cloud Synchronization**: Syncing your workout data across devices securely via Google Firebase.
3. **Reminders & Notifications**: Sending local reminders and streak freeze alerts according to your schedule.
4. **App Improvement**: Ensuring stability, debugging anomalies, and optimizing user experience.

---

### 3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

- **Encryption**: All data transmitted between the app and our backend services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols.
- **Local Storage**: Unsynced local session data is stored securely on your device.
- **Retention**: We retain your data as long as your account remains active. You can request deletion at any time.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services

ConquerONE utilizes the following trusted third-party service providers:
- **Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore)**: Secure user authentication and database storage.
- **Android Health Connect & Apple HealthKit**: On-device health metric synchronization.

---

### 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your data:
- **In-App Data Reset**: You can clear your local workout history or perform a full factory reset at any time via `Settings > Critical Operations`.
- **Account & Cloud Data Deletion**: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data. To request deletion, please email us at **support@conquer-one.app** with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"*. Your request will be fulfilled within 30 business days.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

ConquerONE is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
